Features

Everything in your brain, organized

From local files to a living neural network — your knowledge, your way.

🧠

3D Brain Map

Your notes rendered as neurons in a 3D space. Related notes are connected by synapses based on semantic similarity.

🔎

Semantic Recall

Vector-powered search finds related notes by meaning, not just keywords. Like recalling a memory from your brain.

☁️

Cloud Sync

Your files stay local on your Mac. Content mirrors to the cloud so you can access it from iPhone — read-only, always in sync.

📱

iOS Companion

Browse your brain on the go. 3D Brain Map with touch gestures, note viewer, and search — all from your iPhone.

📄

Multi-Project

Open multiple note folders at once. All projects merge into a single unified brain map for cross-project discovery.

🔗

Slack & Calendar

Connect your Slack workspace and Google Calendar. See conversations and events alongside your notes.

Concept

The Brain Metaphor

SBrain maps familiar concepts to how a brain works.

Traditional SBrain Description
Note Memory A single document file
Chunk Neuron A text segment with its embedding
Connection Synapse Similarity-based link between neurons
Indexing Memorize Scan folders and store in brain
Search Recall Retrieve related memories by meaning
